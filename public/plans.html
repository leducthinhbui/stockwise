Questions about plans

No. Every plan, including Full, only explains holdings and what has changed about them. None of them ever tells you to buy, sell or hold anything — that is a financial product advice service, and StockWise is not one.

Yes. Plans are billed monthly with no lock-in contract, so you can move between Free, Standard and Full, or cancel outright, from your account settings at any time.

The list of holdings you entered is deleted from our systems once your account is closed. StockWise is read-only and was never connected to a trading account, so cancelling never affects any holding you actually own.

Your existing explanations keep updating on their normal schedule. Any holding beyond your plan's limit is shown but not explained until you upgrade or remove another holding to make room for it — nothing is deleted, and nothing renews or charges automatically without you choosing a plan first.

What is included

Holding explanations

What each company or fund does, how it earns, and what its recent announcements actually said.

Change breakdowns

When a balance, dividend or distribution moves, a written explanation of what caused it, after the fact.

Learning guides

Short written guides that appear when the concept turns up in your own account, not as a course to work through.

About the general examples in our guides

Some guides describe how different mixes of assets have behaved historically. These are general, educational examples about categories of investment. They are not tailored to you, they are not a suggested allocation, and nothing in them takes your circumstances into account.
