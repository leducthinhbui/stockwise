About StockWise

What we do, what we deliberately do not do, and how we got here.

Our background

StockWise started in 2021 when one of our founders inherited a share portfolio and realised she could read every number on the statement without understanding a single decision behind it. Nothing she could find explained her own holdings; everything either started from “what is a share” or tried to sell her a product.

We are based in Burwood, Victoria. Everyone on the team uses StockWise on their own portfolio, which is the main reason the explanations stay in plain language.

Where StockWise sits

Three different jobs get confused with one another. This is which one we do.

How StockWise differs from a broker and from a financial adviser
Type of service What its job is How it is paid
A broker Executes the trade you ask for. Brokerage per trade, so more trading earns it more.
A financial adviser Gives you a personal recommendation and carries responsibility for it. Fees for personal advice, which is why it is licensed.
StockWise Explains what your holdings are and what has happened to them. Nothing else. A flat membership fee. We earn the same whether you trade or not.

What we will and will not do

We will

  • Describe what a holding is and how the business or fund actually makes money.
  • Explain, after the event, what caused a change in your account.
  • Define every term we use, without flagging it as remedial.
  • Say plainly when a question needs a licensed adviser or a registered tax agent.

We will not

  • Tell you to buy, sell or hold anything.
  • Score or rate a holding, which is a recommendation wearing a number.
  • Take payment or referral fees from any broker or fund manager.
  • Use countdowns, streaks or “don't miss out” messaging of any kind.
