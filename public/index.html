See what your portfolio is actually telling you

StockWise turns the movements in your existing share portfolio into plain English, so you can understand what you already own.

We explain what is happening. We never tell you what to buy or sell.

StockWise is not a broker and not a financial adviser. We do not execute trades, we do not earn anything when you buy or sell, and we never rate a holding as good or bad. Our only job is to translate what your portfolio and the market are doing into plain English, so the decisions stay yours.

What exactly counts as advice?

Under Australian law, telling a specific person what to do with a specific holding — buy it, sell it, keep it — is financial product advice, and giving it requires an Australian Financial Services Licence. StockWise does not hold one, and does not want to need one.

That is why we describe what a holding is and what has happened to it, but never say whether it is a good one to have. A colour, a score or a rating would answer that question just as clearly as a sentence would, so we do not use those either.

A real example, before you sign up for anything

This is the kind of explanation StockWise produces. No score, no verdict, no recommendation.

“Why did $340 in franking credits appear in my account this quarter?”

What happened
Two of your Australian holdings paid a fully franked dividend in the same quarter. The $340 is the franking credit attached to those dividends, recorded separately from the cash you received.
What a franking credit is
Australian companies pay tax on their profits before paying dividends. A franking credit is your share of the tax already paid, passed on to you so the same profit is not taxed twice.
Where it shows up
It appears on your annual tax statement rather than as cash in your account, which is why the dollar figure does not match your bank balance.
What we are not saying
We are not telling you whether these holdings are worth keeping, or what to do next. That decision is yours, and any tax question is one for a registered tax agent.
